Machine Status Checker

A simple monitoring tool for reviewing the condition of industrial pumps, CNC machines, and compressors.

Purpose

The Machine Status Checker helps operators and maintenance teams quickly inspect machine readings and identify healthy, warning, or critical conditions. It reduces the need to manually compare temperature, vibration, pressure, spindle, and operating data.

What you can check

  • Pump temperature, vibration, pressure, and flow rate
  • CNC spindle temperature, vibration, speed, and tool wear
  • Compressor pressure, temperature, vibration, and operating hours
  • The latest reading for every machine in a CSV sample
